Athayka – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

Athayka ("Athayka", "we", "us") is a mobile app by Anayka (developer name on Google Play: Atharv AI). Athayka connects local shops and local service workers with nearby customers: shopkeepers list their products, stock and prices, and customers scan a shop's QR code or find nearby shops to place orders. This policy explains what information the Athayka app collects, how we use it, and who we share it with.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Mobile number – to create your account and log you in with a one-time password (OTP).
  • Name and email address (if provided) – to show on your profile and orders and to contact you about your account.
  • Delivery addresses – you can save one or more addresses so shops can deliver your orders.
  • Shop details (for shopkeepers) – shop name, address, products, stock, prices, offers and photos you upload.
  • Orders, cart and reviews – items you order, order status, and ratings or reviews you write for shops.

Information collected through the app

  • Location – your approximate or precise location, with your permission, to show shops near you (for example within 3 km) and to help with delivery. You can turn off location permission in your phone settings; some features will then not work.
  • Camera – used only when you scan a shop's QR code or take a photo to upload (for example a product photo). We do not record video or access the camera in the background.
  • Photos – images you choose to upload, such as product or shop photos.
  • Device and app information – app version, device type and basic error logs, to keep the app working and fix problems.

Payments: customers pay the shop directly (for example by UPI to the shop, or cash) after the shop accepts the order. Athayka does not process these payments and does not collect or store card, UPI PIN or bank account details. We record the order's payment status (for example "payment done" by the customer and "payment received" by the shopkeeper).

Shopkeeper wallet: shopkeepers keep a prepaid wallet with Athayka from which Athayka's per-order fee is deducted. For wallet recharges we record the amount, date and payment reference needed to verify the recharge.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account (OTP login).
  • To show nearby shops, their products and prices.
  • To place and track orders, and to send order updates.
  • To let shopkeepers manage their shop, stock, prices and offers.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, and to keep the service safe.
  • To improve the app and fix errors.

We do not sell your personal information. Athayka does not show third-party ads.

3. Who we share information with

  • Shops you order from – your name, mobile number, delivery address and order details, so they can prepare and deliver your order (shops deliver orders themselves).
  • Customers (for shopkeepers) – your shop's public details, products, prices and reviews are visible to app users.
  • Service providers who help us run Athayka, such as cloud hosting, database, SMS (for OTP) and email providers. They process data only on our behalf.
  • Legal reasons – when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users, shops or Athayka.

4. Data security

Data is sent between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account information while your account is active. Order records may be kept for as long as needed for accounting, legal or dispute purposes.

You can ask us to delete your account and personal data at any time by emailing ayka@atharvgenai.in from your registered email, or by writing from the app with your registered mobile number. We will delete or anonymise your data, except where we must keep it by law.

6. Your choices

  • You can update your name and addresses in the app.
  • You can turn off location or camera permission in your phone settings.
  • You can request a copy, correction or deletion of your data by contacting us.

7. Children

Athayka is meant for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the effective date at the top.
